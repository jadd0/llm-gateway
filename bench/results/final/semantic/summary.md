# Semantic cache evaluation

Sampled 6000 QQP and 4000 PAWS pairs (seed 42); dev 5000 pairs (1962 duplicates), test 5000 pairs (1994 duplicates). Every figure below is on test; the threshold was chosen on dev.

How it is measured. *Pairwise* looks at each pair alone. *Nearest neighbour* is how the gateway looks up: the store holds the first prompt of every pair, each second prompt is asked of it, and the nearest entry is the candidate. A served answer is right only when the pair is a duplicate and the entry is that pair's own first prompt (or the identical text); any other entry served counts as wrong. *Guards* refuse a candidate whose numbers, quoted strings, names or negation words differ from the request's, or whose content words differ by more than 2 (symmetric difference). Hit rate is the share of duplicate pairs served right; wrong rate is the share of served answers that are wrong.

Threshold selection: nearest neighbour with guards, on dev: the most right answers served with at most 1.0% of served answers wrong. When no threshold fits that budget the table says so and recommends the threshold with the lowest wrong rate among those that still serve at least 5% of the duplicates.

Embedders: local. Input types: none. Conditions: not recorded.
